Using Naro

Terms of use

Last updated September 22, 2026 · Current development version

Naro is an independent macOS app developed by Sergei Sakharovskii. The app is currently in development; a public installer is not available.

What Naro does

Naro opens supported Office files in Google’s browser-based editors and synchronizes saved changes back to your Mac. You need a compatible Mac, an internet connection, and a Google account with permission to use the relevant services. Your organization may restrict access to third-party apps.

Your files and accounts

Only connect accounts and open files you are authorized to use. You retain ownership of your documents. Using Naro does not give the developer ownership of your content. The app’s data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Development status and limitations

Development builds may contain defects or change behavior. Keep independent backups of important documents. Synchronization requires Naro to remain running and depends on Google saving the file and on network availability. Office compatibility depends on Google’s editors; complex document features may change or be unsupported.

Closing Naro stops synchronization until it runs again. Deleting a local file does not automatically delete the Google Drive copy, and deleting the Drive copy does not delete your local document. Automatic app updates are not yet implemented.
